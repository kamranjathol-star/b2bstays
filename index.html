FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can use this?

Registered travel agents, tour operators and companies in the UK and abroad. We work with the trade only and don’t sell to the public.

Is the portal live?

Not yet — it’s opening soon. Request access and we’ll contact you when your account is ready.

Do you handle visas?

We offer visa services for your clients, subject to Saudi regulations and our licence. Requirements and processing are set by the Saudi authorities and may change.

Which services can I request?

Hotels in Makkah, Madinah, Jeddah and Riyadh; visa services; airport and intercity transfers; transportation; ziyarat and sightseeing; Haramain train tickets; and corporate tours.

What do I need to apply?

Your company and contact details, the services you’re interested in and your expected bookings per month. A trade licence or IATA number is optional — add it if you have one.

How will you contact me?

By email or WhatsApp, using the details in your request.

How is my data used?

To review and reply to your request. See our Privacy Policy for details.
