Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Who we are

This website is operated by the business below (“we”, “us”). We provide business-to-business (B2B) travel services in Saudi Arabia to registered travel agents and companies. This website is not intended for the general public.

2. Information we collect

When you use the “Request access” form we collect:

  • company or trading name, your name, work email address and WhatsApp number;
  • your country, and your trade licence or IATA number if you choose to give one;
  • the services you are interested in and your expected bookings per month;
  • a record of your agreement to be contacted (the wording shown to you, the version of this policy, and the date and time);
  • anti-spam signals: whether a hidden form field was filled in and how long the form took to complete.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record technical information such as your IP address and browser type in server logs, for security and reliability. We do not ask for passwords, payment card details or passport details through this website.
